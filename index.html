Aftercare

Healing Guide

01

Clean & Moisturize

Wash gently with antibacterial soap 2x daily. Apply thin layer of ointment.

02

Don’t Scratch

Itching is normal. Pat or slap the area gently — never pick at scabs.

03

Avoid Sun & Water

No swimming, soaking, or direct sunlight for 2–3 weeks.

04

Stay Hydrated

Healthy skin heals faster. Drink water and avoid alcohol.
